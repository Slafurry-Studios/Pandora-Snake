## ABOUT
Pandora Snake follows Pandora, a clueless witch streamer, and Jormungandr, the legendary World Serpent strapped with a machine gun, as they smash through cities across the multiverse in search of the mysterious Sage. Built for the GAMESEED 2026 competition under the theme *"So Absurd, It's Cool"*, the game reimagines classic Snake as a fast-paced arcade destruction experience—the more chaos you cause, the more subscribers you gain, and the crazier the run gets.

---

## FEATURES
- **Destroy Everything** — Smash buildings and devour humans or items to fill the Threat Point bar
- **Eat Consumables** — Destroyed buildings drop items with a chance to heal the player
- **Grow Longer** — Eat to grow Jormungandr's body and see how far you can stretch
- **Gain Subscribers** — Cause destruction and complete objectives to earn Subscription Points
- **Choose Upgrades** — Pick from 15+ upgrade cards as you progress, unlocked by subscriber milestones
- **Fight the Final Boss** — Survive the chaos long enough to face what's waiting at the end of the road
